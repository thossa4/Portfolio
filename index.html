About Me

Turning Geospatial Data
Into Actionable Insight

I am a PhD candidate in Geography at Louisiana State University specializing in GIS, Remote Sensing, and machine learning for coastal hazard and climate risk assessment. My research spans multi-hazard susceptibility mapping, drought prediction, evapotranspiration modeling, and climate migration — producing 5 journal manuscripts and 5 book chapters at various stages from in-review to in-press.

As a Teaching Assistant at LSU and SCAUG Scholarship 2024 recipient, I combine rigorous academic training with hands-on expertise in ArcGIS Pro, Google Earth Engine, Python, and deep learning frameworks (XGBoost, LSTM, 1D-CNN). I collaborate with LSU AgCenter's LaHouse Research Center on agricultural and coastal resilience research.

My dissertation core paper benchmarks 8 ML/DL algorithms (XGBoost, RF, GBM, SVM, MLP, 1D-CNN, LSTM, CNN-LSTM) for simultaneous susceptibility mapping of four compound coastal hazards — flooding, subsidence, storm surge, and salinity intrusion — across Terrebonne Parish at 30-m resolution, using 5-fold spatial block cross-validation to test genuine geographic generalizability beyond standard holdout metrics (analysis in final revision).

Multi-Hazard Coastal Susceptibility Mapping — Deltaic Louisiana

Hossain, T. et al.

Simultaneous susceptibility mapping of flooding, land subsidence, storm surge, and salinity intrusion across Terrebonne Parish using 8 ML/DL algorithms (XGBoost, RF, GBM, SVM, MLP, 1D-CNN, LSTM, CNN-LSTM) and 65 conditioning factors at 30-m resolution, with 5-fold spatial block cross-validation to expose real-world generalizability that conventional holdout metrics conceal (final figures in revision).

Ensemble ML Drought Susceptibility Mapping — Louisiana

Hossain, T., Mostafiz, R.B. et al.

High-resolution (30-m) statewide drought susceptibility framework integrating Landsat 8 OLI and 15 environmental variables. XGBoost was the strongest individual regressor (R² ≈ 0.93), clearly ahead of Random Forest, ANN, and SVM. Hotspot analysis identifies agricultural and hydrological drought clusters across Louisiana.

Climate-Induced Migration & Agricultural Resilience — Coastal Louisiana

Mostafiz, R.B. & Hossain, T. — Climate on the Move

Parish-year panel analysis (2013–2018, n=11 parishes) linking storms, drought (DSCI), and saltwater intrusion (CRMS) to Louisiana sugarcane yield losses. Fixed-effects regression with USDA NASS and NOAA Storm Events data quantifies the hazard–agriculture–mobility pathway.

Climate MigrationSugarcanePanel DataCoastal Louisiana
Book Chapter · In Press

ML-Driven ET₀ Modeling for Precision Irrigation — Louisiana

Mostafiz, R.B. & Hossain, T. — Precision Irrigation in Humid Climates

Comprehensive ET₀ framework comparing FAO-56 Penman-Monteith, Hargreaves-Samani, Priestley-Taylor, and Turc against RF, XGBoost, ANN, LSTM, and 1D-CNN, augmented with NASA MODIS/VIIRS, NOAA GOES-16, and NASA POWER GeoAI inputs across Louisiana's AERIS network.

ET₀ ModelingDeep LearningGeoAIPrecision Agriculture

Multi-Hazard Tropical Cyclone Risk — Bay of Bengal

Hossain, T. & Mostafiz, R.B.

Multi-scale resilience framework for compound cyclone hazard governance across Bangladesh, India, and Myanmar. Case studies of Cyclones Sidr (2007), Amphan (2020), and Mocha (2023) demonstrate how temporal hazard convergence — not peak intensity — drives worst-case outcomes.

Rapid review of AI and GIS applications for disaster prediction and resilience in humanitarian engineering. Introduces the Technology-Context Fit (TCF) Matrix — an original evaluative typology classifying humanitarian AI deployments by technical and contextual readiness.
